## Problem Statement

### The Disenfranchised Grief Problem

Pet loss triggers genuine grief responses similar to losing a family member, yet 97% of pet owners report their grief is dismissed by friends or family with comments like "it was just a dog." This creates a "validation void" where owners experience profound loss without societal structures to legitimize their mourning.

### The Soul Pet Phenomenon

Many describe their pet as an irreplaceable "soulmate"—the only companion during depression, life transitions, or isolation. When these pets pass, owners face identity threat and describe the loss as "my whole world is different." Traditional grief resources don't address this depth of attachment.

### Specific Pain Points During Acute Grief

| Pain Point | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| **Emotional Overwhelm** | Writing a eulogy feels impossible when grieving; organizing photos takes energy they don't have |
| **Euthanasia Guilt** | Paralyzing uncertainty about timing—"did I wait too long or not long enough?" Need logical validation that their decision was compassionate |
| **Routine Disruption** | The "silence" at bedtime, empty feeding times, no greeting at the door. Daily reminders trigger acute grief |
| **Need for Evidence** | Collecting fur, whiskers, thousands of photos to "prove the pet existed." Fear of forgetting quirks and personality details |
| **Workplace Dismissal** | Feeling "betrayed" when employers don't acknowledge the loss or expect immediate return to normal productivity |
| **Social Media Inadequacy** | A single Facebook post disappears in feeds and fails to capture their pet's life story |
| **No Central Hub** | Friends and family want to share memories but have no structured place to do so |
| **Outdated Solutions** | Legacy memorial sites like Rainbow Bridge have 2005-era UX and feel impersonal |
| **Expensive Alternatives** | Custom portraits ($200-500) are beautiful but lack the narrative and community elements |

### Current Market Gap

While Pet2AI offers $6.99 AI portraits, they deliver static downloads with no permanent page, no community features, and no ongoing service. There's no modern platform that combines AI-assisted storytelling, collaborative memory collection, and permanent hosting at an accessible price point.

## Core Features (MVP)

### 1. Memorial Creation Flow

**User Journey:**
1. Land on homepage with immediate CTA: "Create a Memorial"
2. Enter pet's name, species, birth/death dates (optional)
3. Upload 5-20 photos (with smart cropping suggestions)
4. Answer 5-7 AI-guided prompts to generate eulogy content
5. Preview generated memorial page
6. Edit eulogy and photo arrangement if desired
7. Purchase to unlock permanent memorial + memory wall

**Key Features:**

**AI Eulogy Assistant:** Two modes:
1. *Narrative mode* with conversational prompts that feel therapeutic ("What was their favorite thing to do?" "What quirk made you laugh?"), generates 3-4 paragraph eulogy (250-400 words)
2. *Decision Support mode* helps with euthanasia guilt by analyzing symptoms/medical context to reframe the decision as "compassionate timing" rather than "giving up"

**Smart Photo Timeline:** AI analyzes EXIF data for chronological order, but also uses vision AI (Gemini Flash) to identify:
- Physical changes over time ("white hair around her mouth", aging progression)
- Habits and patterns ("favorite sunny spot", "making biscuits on blanket", "by your side during work calls")
- Connection moments (eye contact, lap time, play)
- Auto-generates captions like "Your morning sun-bather" that witness the pet's unique personality

**Dignity-First Design:** 3-5 tasteful templates with muted tones and elegant typography. NO party aesthetics or bright colors. Think: museum gallery, not birthday card.

**Physical Artifact Archive:** Section for uploading photos of physical keepsakes: collar, urn, favorite toys, fur clippings, paw prints, shrine. Acts as "metadata hub" backing up physical memories digitally. Special "Collar Archive" sub-section documenting where they wore it, the jingle it made.

**Mobile-First:** 80% of users will create on mobile during emotional moments. Touch-optimized photo upload, one-handed navigation.

### 2. Memorial Page

**Components:**
- Hero section with pet's name, dates, and primary photo
- AI-generated eulogy (editable by creator)
- Photo gallery with timeline view
- Collaborative Memory Wall (see detailed section below)
- Shareable link (rainbowbridge.pet/petname-year)
- Virtual candle lighting (visitors can light candle, see total count)

**Technical Requirements:**
- Fast load times (<2 seconds)—pages will be shared widely
- Beautiful OG tags for social sharing (title, description, hero image)
- Print-friendly CSS for physical keepsakes
- Permanent hosting (99.9% uptime SLA)
- SEO optimized (each memorial page indexed for pet name + breed searches)

### 3. Collaborative Memory Wall (PRIMARY DIFFERENTIATOR)

**Strategic Importance:** This feature is the core of our viral growth engine and what justifies our $49-99 price vs Pet2AI's $6.99 downloads. We're not selling a product; we're providing an ongoing service that brings people together around shared loss.

**Core Functionality:**
- **Open Contribution:** Anyone with the memorial link can add written memories, photos, or videos (with creator moderation)
- **Guided Prompts:** Contributors see prompts like "Share a favorite memory" or "What made [Pet Name] special?" to reduce blank-page anxiety
- **Rich Media:** Support for text (up to 500 words), photos (up to 10 per contribution), and short videos (up to 2 min)
- **Chronological Display:** Memories appear in timeline order, creating a living archive
- **Contributor Attribution:** Each memory shows contributor name and date, honoring their participation
- **Group Gift Functionality:** Contributors can collectively purchase physical products (memory book, canvas print) as group gift. "12 people contributed $8 each to gift you this memory book"—formalizes the supportive community acts.

**Moderation Workflows (CRITICAL):**
- **Pre-Moderation Default:** All contributions go to pending queue by default, creator approves before they appear publicly
- **Email Notifications:** Creator receives email when new memory submitted: "Sarah shared a memory of Max. Review and approve?"
- **Quick Actions:** Creator can approve, edit (fix typos), request revision, or delete from email or dashboard
- **Auto-Approve Option:** Creator can toggle "auto-approve from close friends" list for trusted contributors
- **Grace Period:** Even auto-approved memories have 24hr window where creator can unpublish

**Viral Mechanics:**
- **Invitation Flow:** Creator shares memorial link via email, text, or social media with message: "I created a memorial for Max. Add your memories here: [link]"
- **Contributor Exposure:** After contributing, user sees banner: "When you lose a pet, PetMemorial.ai is here for you too. Create memorial [CTA]"
- **Social Proof:** Memorial page shows "12 people have shared memories" creating FOMO and encouraging participation
- **Network Effect:** Average memorial shared with 20-30 people. If 15% of contributors become creators within 90 days, we achieve viral coefficient >1.0
- **Email Capture:** Contributors provide email to receive notifications when new memories added (builds audience for future pet loss)

**Design Philosophy:**
- Dignity over celebration—muted colors, elegant typography, no confetti or party elements
- Inspired by museum guestbooks, not Kudoboard birthday cards
- Texture: subtle paper texture background, soft shadows, handwritten font for contributor names
- Hierarchy: Creator's eulogy prominently featured at top, memory wall flows below

**B2B/Workplace Integration:**
- **Dignity in the Workplace:** Employees feel "betrayed" when employers ignore pet loss. Position as structured grief support that prevents presenteeism and validates the loss.
- **Slack Integration:** Post memorial link to #team-general channel, colleagues can contribute without leaving Slack
- **Teams Integration:** Similar workflow for Microsoft Teams environments
- **Vet Staff Compassion Fatigue:** Veterinary staff suffer PTSD from traumatic deaths. Memorial pages let them see pet's "life story" beyond clinical symptoms, serving as debriefing tool.
- **HR Dashboard:** Aggregate anonymous data showing employee engagement with grief support tools (for benefits reporting)
- **Pricing Model:** $500-2000/year for company-wide access (unlimited employee memorials)

### 4. Physical Products (Print-on-Demand)

- **Memory Book:** 20-page printed book (8x10) with eulogy, photos, and top 10 contributed memories. $79 upgrade at checkout, $99 if purchased later. Printed via Gelato for global fulfillment.
- **Canvas Print:** 16x20 featured photo with eulogy text overlaid ($49). Archival quality, gallery-wrapped.
- **QR Code Memorial Plaque:** Engraved plaque with QR code linking to memorial page ($29). Weatherproof for outdoor placement.

**Implementation:** Gelato API for production, Printful as backup. Margins: 40-60% after COGS.

## Technical Architecture

### Tech Stack

| Layer | Technology |
|-------|------------|
| Frontend | Next.js 14+ (App Router), TailwindCSS, Shadcn/ui, Framer Motion for subtle animations |
| Backend | Next.js API routes, Supabase (PostgreSQL + Storage), Row Level Security for privacy |

| AI | OpenAI GPT-4o for eulogy generation, Claude Sonnet for image captions, Gemini Flash for high-volume vision tasks |
| Payments | Stripe Checkout + Customer Portal for subscription management |
| Print-on-Demand | Gelato API (primary), Printful (backup) |
| Hosting | Vercel (frontend), Supabase (backend/DB), Cloudflare R2 (image CDN) |
| Email | Resend (for moderation notifications, share invites) |
| Analytics | PostHog (product analytics, feature flags, session replay) |

### Database Schema

**Key Tables:**

```sql
users: id, email, clerk_id, created_at

memorials: id, user_id, pet_name, slug, birth_date, death_date, eulogy,
           decision_support_used, template, is_paid, is_published,
           auto_approve_memories, created_at, updated_at

photos: id, memorial_id, url, caption, ai_detected_tags (habits, physical_changes, moments),
        sort_order, uploaded_by, created_at

artifacts: id, memorial_id, artifact_type (collar, urn, toy, fur, paw_print, shrine),
           photo_url, description, collar_story (if type=collar), created_at

memories: id, memorial_id, contributor_name, contributor_email, content,
          photo_urls, is_approved, moderation_status, created_at, approved_at

group_gifts: id, memorial_id, product_type, total_amount, participant_count,
             stripe_payment_ids, status, created_at

contributors: id, memorial_id, email, name, became_creator (boolean), created_at

anniversary_events: id, memorial_id, event_type (1_year, 2_year),
                    summary_data (visitors, memories, candles), sent_at, created_at

payments: id, memorial_id, user_id, stripe_payment_id, amount, product_type,
          status, created_at
```

## Risks & Mitigation

### Brand & Emotional Risks

**Risk:** Being perceived as "predatory" or exploiting grief. The pet loss community is extremely sensitive to high-markup products (China-made urns at 4000% markup, emergency vets charging $1300 for cremation). We must be seen as a "healer" not a "transactional provider."

**Mitigation:**
- Transparency-First Pricing: All prices clearly stated upfront, no hidden fees, no dark patterns
- Free Preview: 7-day trial lets users evaluate value before financial commitment
- No Pressure Tactics: No countdown timers, no pop-ups, no "limited time" scarcity tactics
- Authentic Founder Story: Share personal connection to pet loss in marketing (builds trust)
- Community-First Approach: Position as grief support tool, not revenue optimization machine

### Legal & Compliance Risks

**Risk:** Veterinarian referral commissions for death-care services are prohibited in many US jurisdictions (e.g., Washington RCW 18.39.145 bans "finders fees").

**Mitigation:**
- SaaS Model for Vets: Offer free co-branded portal as value-add for clients instead of per-lead commission
- Educational Materials: Provide vets with grief support resources, position as client care tool
- Direct Consumer Focus: Prioritize organic consumer acquisition over B2B partnerships to avoid regulatory issues
- Legal Review: Consult with attorney familiar with funeral/pet aftercare regulations before scaling B2B

### Technical & AI Quality Risks

**Risk:** If AI-generated eulogies feel generic, repetitive, or obviously AI-written, perceived value collapses. Owners know their pets "better than anyone" and will reject inaccurate or generic content.

**Mitigation:**
- Position AI as Collaborator: Not a replacement for human emotion, but a tool to help owners "get every single thing off their chest" before transitioning to life without pet.
- Extensive Prompt Engineering: Test 50+ eulogy variations before launch to ensure authentic voice
- Specific Details Required: Force users to provide concrete examples ("He always barked at the mailman") to avoid generic output
- Human-in-the-Loop: Always allow editing before final publish—users can refine AI output
- A/B Testing: Test different AI models (GPT-4o vs Claude Opus) to find best emotional resonance
- Continuous Improvement: Collect user feedback on eulogy quality, iterate prompts monthly

### Conversion & Growth Risks

**Risk:** 30% preview-to-paid conversion is aggressive assumption. May be lower (15-20%) in reality, requiring 2x more traffic to hit $20k MRR.

**Mitigation:**
- Backup Pricing Tier: If conversion is <25%, add $29 "Basic Digital Only" tier to capture price-sensitive users
- Exit Intent Offers: Show one-time discount (10% off) if user tries to leave preview without purchasing
- Email Follow-up: Send reminder email on Day 5 of preview: "Your memorial expires in 2 days" with purchase link
- Testimonials & Social Proof: Feature customer stories on pricing page to build trust
- Optimize Viral Mechanics: If organic conversion is low, double down on memory wall features to drive contributor-to-creator loop

### Competitive & Market Saturation Risks

**Risk:** If this model works, well-funded copycats (Pet2AI, Crown & Paw) will add collaborative features within 6 months.

**Mitigation:**
- Build in Public: Establish brand early as "the founder who gets it" through authentic community engagement
- Network Effects: Memory wall creates switching costs—users can't easily migrate collected memories to competitor
- Speed to Market: Launch MVP in 5 weeks, gain 1000+ users before competitors notice
- B2B Partnerships: Lock in vet/crematorium partnerships early to control distribution channel
- Emotional Moat: Users won't switch once they've created memorial for deceased pet (high switching costs emotionally)
